# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Members of a student robotics team using shared laptops in the workshop to find parts and understand where they belong.

## Product Purpose

Retriever makes the team's physical inventory searchable by connecting every part to a precise, human-readable storage address. Success means a team member can find a part in seconds without asking someone who already knows the shop.

## Brand Personality

Friendly, capable, and straightforward. The interface should feel welcoming to new members while remaining fast for experienced users. Its visual identity is grounded in the Rotationaries' deep maroon and clean white team colors.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic enterprise admin dashboards, overly technical warehouse software, decorative sci-fi styling, and interfaces that hide common actions behind dense menus.

## Design Principles

- Put finding a part ahead of managing the system.
- Translate structured locations into directions a person can follow.
- Make the five real storage areas recognizable throughout the interface.
- Use plain language that works for new and experienced team members.
- Keep frequent actions visible, quick, and forgiving.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, complete keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and labels that do not depend on color alone.
